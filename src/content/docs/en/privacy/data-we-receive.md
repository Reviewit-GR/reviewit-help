This page sums up what happens to your customer's details when you send an invitation. The full information is in the [Privacy Policy](https://www.reviewit.gr/privacy-policy/) (in Greek).

## What we receive {#what-we-receive}

From each invitation: the **email**, the **first name** (if you send it) and the **order number**. Nothing else from the order: no products, no amounts, no address.

## What we do with it {#what-we-do}

We use it only to send one invitation to write a review on behalf of your business. We do not use it for advertising and we do not send it to third parties.

## How long we keep it {#retention}

- **The email and the name are deleted as soon as the message is sent**, and at the latest within 30 days if it cannot be sent.
- We keep an **irreversible code** (a hash) of the address, not the address. It helps us not to send to the same customer again for 90 days and to respect anyone who asked not to receive invitations. The address cannot be recovered from it.
- When an invitation arrives, we check whether the address already has an account with a review of your business, so that we do not send a pointless invitation. We keep nothing from this check, other than the fact that the invitation was skipped.
- The **order number** stays with the invitation, so that a second one is not sent for the same order.

## The call log {#call-log}

The «Recent calls» (Πρόσφατες κλήσεις) on the «My business» page show what your shop asked for and what we answered: time, whether it was made with the test key, response code and order number. **They contain no customer email or name** and are deleted after 30 days.

## What you need to do {#what-you-do}

Tell your customers that they will receive an invitation to write a review in their email. That is why we ask for a confirmation when you turn on invitations. And do not send the addresses of customers who have objected.
