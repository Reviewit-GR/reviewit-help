Every sample sends one invitation and prints the response. Replace `YOUR_API_KEY` with your key. **Start with the [test key](/en/connect/get-started/#test-key)**: you will get `202` and `"test": true`, and no email is sent.

The language you choose stays chosen on all the pages.

The details (`customer@example.gr`, `Maria`, `1001`) are made up. Send your own.

## Things to note {#things-to-note}

- **Call it after the order is completed**, and do not let an error or a slow answer stop the customer's order. All the samples have a time limit of 10 seconds.
- **The key stays on the server.** Never in code that runs in the customer's browser.
- **Retrying is safe:** the same order is not sent twice.
- For the response codes, see [responses and errors](/en/connect/responses-and-errors/).
