---
title: Inviting customers to review
description: How Reviewit sends your customers an email inviting them to write a review, and which rules apply.
---

An invitation is an email that we send, on behalf of your business, to your customer. It has a «Write a review» button. Anyone who writes a review from that email appears as a verified buyer.

The Reviewit site is in Greek, so the names of its pages and buttons are given in English with the Greek in brackets.

## Three ways to send it {#three-ways}

| Way | For whom | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **One invitation or a CSV file** | Businesses without an online shop, such as a gym or a clinic | [Send with a form or a CSV file](/en/business/send-csv/) |
| **Automatically from your shop** | Online shops | [Connect your shop](/en/connect/get-started/) |
| **A link or a QR code** | Anyone who wants to put a link on invoices, receipts or packaging | On the «My business» page (Η επιχείρησή μου), in the «Collecting reviews» section (Συλλογή κριτικών) |

The link and the QR code send no email and give us no personal data: the customer writes the review on their own.

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

1. Your business must have an active plan.
2. An owner opens **My business → Collecting reviews**, turns on «Send invitations» (Αποστολή προσκλήσεων) and confirms that they tell their customers they will receive an invitation to review. The confirmation is asked once.

Owners and managers can send invitations. Only owners manage the settings and the keys.

## What you send us {#what-you-send}

Only three things:

- the customer's **email**,
- their **first name** (optional),
- the **order number** (optional when you send from the page: if it is missing, we make one).

We need nothing else from the order. See [what we do with this data](/en/privacy/data-we-receive/).

## Rules {#rules}

- **Delay.** The email is sent a while after the order. You set how many hours. If you set nothing, it is 24.
- **One invitation per order.** If you send the same order again, a second email is not sent.
- **No repeats.** The same customer is not invited again by the same business for 90 days.
- **Not to anyone who has already written a review.** Each account writes one review per business, so we do not invite a customer who already has a review of your business. They can change or delete it from their account.
- **The customer can refuse.** Every email has an «I do not want more invitations» link. Whoever clicks it receives no invitation from any business.
- **A monthly limit by plan.**

| Plan | Invitations per month |
|---|---|
| Basic | 300 |
| Pro | 1000 |
| Premium | 5000 |
| Enterprise | no limit |

On the «My business» page you can see how many invitations you have left this month.
