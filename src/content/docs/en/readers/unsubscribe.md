---
title: I do not want more invitations
description: How to stop receiving invitations to write a review from Reviewit, for all businesses.
---

You received an email from Reviewit inviting you to write a review of a business, and you want no more?

1. Open the email and click the link at the end of it that says you do not want more invitations.
2. The page that opens asks you to confirm. Confirm.

From that moment you **will not receive an invitation from any business**, not only from the one that sent you the email. Whatever was already queued for you is cancelled, and your address is deleted from the invitations. We keep only an irreversible code of it, so that we do not send to you again.

If you do not have the email, or the link does not work, write to us at [help@reviewit.gr](mailto:help@reviewit.gr).
